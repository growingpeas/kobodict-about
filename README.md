# Glossmark

**Turn your word list into a dictionary your e-reader opens, made on your phone.**

Language learners keep vocabulary lists, fantasy readers keep glossaries of names and places, students keep the terms of a course. Making a custom e-reader dictionary out of them usually means a computer and a command-line tool. Glossmark builds it on your phone.

## What it does

- Import a CSV or TSV word list, paste lines like "word = definition", or type words by hand
- Pick which column holds the word and which holds the definition; the header row is detected
- Every alphabet: accents, Cyrillic, Greek, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, apostrophes and multi-word entries
- Choose the language code (fr, de, en-fr for a translation dictionary)
- Build a dicthtml-xx.zip dictionary in the e-reader's own format, then save or share it
- Copy it into the .kobo/custom-dict folder with a computer, or straight from the phone with a USB OTG cable
- A sample glossary is built in, to try it without any file

## Included without Pro

Everything above, for dictionaries of up to 200 entries.

## Pro: one purchase, yours for good

- Dictionaries of any size
- Several saved glossaries, one per book or subject
- Other forms: plurals and conjugations that open the same entry
- Formatting: bold, italic and line breaks in definitions

No subscription, no ads, no account.

## Privacy

Your word lists stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and Glossmark asks for no storage permission.

Works with Kobo e-readers running firmware 4.24 or later, which read custom dictionaries from .kobo/custom-dict.
